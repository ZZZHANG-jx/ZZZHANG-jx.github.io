I am a researcher working on **document image analysis**, **optical character recognition (OCR)**, and **multimodal document understanding**. My goal is to make machines read the way people do — handling a document that is photographed at an angle, folded, shadowed, or written in an unfamiliar layout, and turning it into structured, machine-readable content.

My recent work spans document image restoration and dewarping (**DocRes**, CVPR 2024; **Marior**), unified pixel-level OCR (**UPOCR**, ICML 2024), large multimodal models for visual document understanding (**Dockylin**, AAAI 2025), and datasets and benchmarks that push the field forward (**M6Doc**, CVPR 2023; **OCRGenBench**). Several of these are widely used as standard baselines for document AI.

I did my research training at South China University of Technology, and I currently work as a researcher in industry.

## Research Interests

- Document image restoration, dewarping, and appearance enhancement
- Document layout analysis and table structure recognition
- Unified OCR: detection, recognition, and segmentation in one interface
- Multimodal large language models for visual document understanding
- Efficient visual token compression for document MLLMs
